What You Can Now Do

  • Describe an ATLO campaign's structure and locate any spacecraft subsystem's critical test points
  • Build a real campaign schedule: work breakdown structure, dependencies, critical path
  • Author and validate a test procedure, and define pass/fail criteria before a test ever runs

Where This Goes From Here

This course was built with a specific focus: working directly on real spacecraft hardware. If that's the direction you want, here's where this content points most directly:

ATLO Engineer / Systems Engineer (Level 1–2)
At satellite manufacturers and prime contractors
Integration & Test Engineer
At aerospace and defense companies
Test Conductor
For environmental test campaigns
Production Engineer
In spacecraft manufacturing environments

None of that means spacecraft work is the only valid path from here. The habits you practiced in this course, reading a schematic before trusting it, verifying before mating, documenting what actually happened instead of what you expected, carry over to plenty of other engineering work. Go wherever you want. This course just aimed squarely at one destination.
