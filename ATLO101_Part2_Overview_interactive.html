Part 2 — Chapter Overview

Execution: Now the Bolts Turn, Now the Telemetry Flows

Part 1 built the schedule and wrote the procedures. Part 2 is where that plan actually meets the hardware -- one verified connection, one torqued fastener, one passed test at a time.
Thirteen steps stand between an approved Part 1 Capstone and a vehicle cleared to ship. Here's the path.
