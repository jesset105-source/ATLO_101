Why Sequence Matters

GEVS-7000 (Lesson 2.6) defines a standard order for these tests, and that order is deliberate. A functional test (Lesson 2.4) runs both before and after each environmental test, specifically so any change caused by that exposure is caught by comparison to baseline — not just checked against a fixed spec. Running tests out of sequence, or skipping the functional test in between, breaks that comparison entirely.

Connecting Back to Thermal Control

Thermal vacuum testing directly exercises the thermal control subsystem covered in Lesson F.6 — heaters cycling on and off, thermistors reporting temperature, insulation (MLI) performing exactly as designed. If a thermal circuit's CSTM check (Lesson 2.1's worked example) had passed with a marginal but in-spec reading, TVAC is often where a marginal component actually reveals a real problem under thermal extremes.
