Part 1 Capstone Assignment

You have completed the Planning module. You understand how an ATLO campaign is structured, how a schedule is built, how procedures are developed, and what test planning requires. Now you are going to apply all of it.

Overview

Build a Detailed ATLO Schedule. Your assignment is to design and develop a detailed ATLO campaign schedule for a fictional small spacecraft. Your schedule must cover three subsystem functional tests of your choice, followed by a system-level functional test that integrates all three.

GATE

This assignment must be reviewed and approved before you are cleared to proceed to Part 2: Execution.

Subsystem Options

Choose any three of the following:

  • Power Subsystem (EPS)
  • Command & Data Handling (C&DH)
  • Communications (RF/Comms)
  • GNC
  • Thermal Control
  • Structures & Mechanisms
  • Propulsion

Submission

Submit your schedule as a document, spreadsheet, or diagram -- whichever format best communicates your plan. There is no single correct answer. A schedule that is logically sound, well-reasoned, and reflects an understanding of how ATLO campaigns actually work will receive full credit. Your submission will be reviewed and approved before you are cleared to proceed to Part 2: Execution.
